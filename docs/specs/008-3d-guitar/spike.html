New: a sounding string vibrates along its whole length over the neck, from the nut to the end, wherever it is fretted, with a soft blur around it. Each pluck restarts it and it dies away with the note. It is slowed right down so the eye can follow it, and higher notes wobble faster. An open string is a hollow ring. 2D starts on the whole neck and can zoom and pan, but never tilts. Same loop: C, G, Am, F, a riff at the 5th fret, then Em. Reply in the terminal.
